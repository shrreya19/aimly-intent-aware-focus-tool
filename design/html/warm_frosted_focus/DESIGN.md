## Brand & Style

This design system embodies mindful intentionality, calm focus, and positive reinforcement. Rather than enforcing punitive site-blocking, the UI creates an environment of warm clarity and gentle guidance. The aesthetic pairs modern frosted-glass surfaces with high-energy radiant hues to make productivity feel uplifting rather than disciplinary.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Modern Warm Glassmorphism:** Translucent glass plates suspended above soft, organic, atmospheric radiant gradients.
- **Atmospheric Visual Depth:** Deep background warmth generated by blurred radial amber and orange gradients, never literal foliage or botanical motifs. Visual metaphors are strictly abstract: geometric rings, linear progress, calibrated scales, and numeric tracking.
- **Confident Typographic Punch:** Uppercase, tight-tracking structural headings contrasted with oversized, monospaced tabular numerals that anchor focus sessions.
- **Tactile Soft Geometry:** Continuous curvaceous geometry across pill-shaped interactive elements and generously rounded structural containers.

## Colors

The palette is strictly calibrated around solar warmth and high-contrast ink neutrality. Red is completely eliminated from the visual lexicon; all alert and drift indicators rely exclusively on warning amber.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Orange (`#FF7A21`):** Primary interactive calls-to-action, active progress arcs, timer track indicators, and focus highlights. Paired exclusively with Ink (`#1E1E1E`) typography for WCAG AA compliance.
- **Amber (`#FFB422`):** Warning states, secondary analytical tracks, and the dedicated "Drift Detected" state. Paired with Ink (`#1E1E1E`) typography.
- **Tertiary Soft Orange Tint (`#FFE3CF`):** Tonal badge fills, hover envelopes, secondary chip containers, and tertiary button fills.
- **Ink Neutral (`#1E1E1E`):** Core body typography, dark inverted buttons, and structural icons. Inverted surfaces strictly take pure white (`#FFFFFF`) text.
- **Cream Foundation (`#F3E6D3`):** Canvas background tone, infused with diffuse radial blurs of `#FF7A21` and `#FFB422` at 15–25% opacity.
- **Frosted Glass Matrix:** Surface fills use `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)` supported by crisp specular highlights of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.80)` along a 1px border.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels to create an aerodynamic, geometric, and warm personality.

### Typographic Rules
- **Structural Hero Headers (`headline-hero`, `headline-lg`):** Must be rendered uppercase with slight positive letter spacing (`0.04em`–`0.06em`) to produce clear architectural hierarchy.
- **Numeric Tracking (`display-timer`, `stat-counter`):** Always enable tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) with tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.04em`) to prevent layout shift during countdowns and active focus intervals.
- **Reading Hierarchy:** Body copy avoids harsh contrast drops, resting comfortably in Ink (`#1E1E1E`) at 400 and 500 weights over the frosted and cream backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

The shell architecture employs an asymmetrical layout optimized for single-task clarity and ambient monitoring.

### Spatial System
- **Shell Layout:** A persistent 72px left-hand icon sidebar containing central interaction anchors, matched with a 64px top application bar holding quick session search, session state pills, and user profile badges.
- **Canvas Behavior:** Below the top bar, content flows inside a fluid 12-column grid system restricted to a maximum container width of `1280px` to maintain focused visual scope.
- **Breakpoints & Adaptations:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** Fixed 72px left rail, top bar pinned, 12-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` outer margins.
  - **Tablet (768px–1023px):** Compact 64px left rail, 8-column layout, `1rem` gutters, and `1.5rem` outer margins.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** Left rail transforms into a bottom glass dock (height 64px). Layout collapses to a 4-column flow with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` margins.
- **Atmospheric Backdrop:** The canvas layer includes two pinned decorative background blurs (300px–500px diameter, `filter: blur(80px)`) positioned at top-right and bottom-left, using `#FF7A21` and `#FFB422` at 18% opacity over the `#F3E6D3` surface.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained via glass transparency and warm ambient refraction rather than heavy opaque drop shadows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (Level 0):** Pure `#F3E6D3` substrate overlaid with organic radiant orange/amber atmospheric blurs.
- **Frosted Surfaces (Level 1 - Cards, Main Containers):**
  - Fill: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)`
  - Backdrop Blur: `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`
  - Border: 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.70)`
  - Shadow: `0 8px 32px 0 rgba(255, 122, 33, 0.08)`, creating a subtle sunlit underglow.
- **Floating Controls (Level 2 - Popovers, Dropdowns, Drift Alerts):**
  - Fill: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`
  - Backdrop Blur: `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`
  - Border: 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.90)`
  - Shadow: `0 12px 40px 0 rgba(30, 30, 30, 0.08), 0 2px 8px 0 rgba(255, 122, 33, 0.06)`
- **Active Radiant Elements (Level 3 - Timers, Rings):**
  - Elements carrying the `#FF7A21` fill emit an energetic ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px 0 rgba(255, 122, 33, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The design system uses pill geometry and generous card radiuses to eliminate aggressive corners, promoting calm focus.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Interactive Controls:** All buttons, status chips, notification tags, and input wrappers utilize continuous pill curvature (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Cards & Surface Modules:** Content containers feature a uniform 24px corner radius (`rounded-3xl` / 1.5rem), providing a smooth frame for the blurred backdrop.
- **Iconography Enclosures:** Circular bounding boxes (`border-radius: 50%`) for avatar frames, sidebar icon backplates, and quick-action icon buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill:** Background `#FF7A21`, text `#1E1E1E`, font weight 600, padding `12px 24px`. Hover state darkens slightly to `#F06B10` with a subtle elevation boost.
- **Secondary Dark Pill:** Background `#1E1E1E`, text `#FFFFFF`, font weight 600, padding `12px 24px`. Hover lifts background to `#2C2C2C`.
- **Subtle Pill:** Background `#FFE3CF`, text `#1E1E1E`, font weight 600, padding `12px 24px`. Hover transitions to `rgba(255, 227, 207, 0.80)`.

### Drift Warning Badge
- **Style:** Pill badge using Amber background (`#FFB422`), Ink text (`#1E1E1E`), uppercase label `label-caps` ("DRIFT DETECTED" or "OFF-AIM").
- **Treatment:** Includes a solid 6px circular dot indicator in `#1E1E1E` pulsing at a relaxed 2-second interval. No red tones are ever introduced.

### Focus Timer Ring & Session Hero
- **Progress Track:** Circular SVG track with an empty baseline of `rgba(30, 30, 30, 0.08)` and active meter rendered in `#FF7A21` with round caps.
- **Active State:** Progress head features a localized ambient bloom (`drop-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(255, 122, 33, 0.5)`).
- **Core Readout:** Centered `display-timer` tabular typography directly displaying minutes and seconds remaining, paired with an uppercase one-line aim label above.

### Frosted Content Cards
- **Specs:** 24px rounded borders, filled with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)`, blurred with `16px` backdrop filter, framed by a 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.70)` border.
- **Content:** Header, metric counters, and clean horizontal separator dividers rendered in `rgba(30, 30, 30, 0.06)`.

### Form Inputs & Search
- **Container:** Pill-shaped wrapper with background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.50)`, border 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.80)`, text `#1E1E1E`, and placeholder `rgba(30, 30, 30, 0.40)`.
- **Focus Ring:** Replaces outer border with a continuous 2px glow ring in `#FF7A21`.

### Navigation Elements
- **Sidebar:** Frosted column (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45)`) with vertical icon stacks. Active tabs feature a pill indicator in `#FFE3CF` with an active `#FF7A21` glyph.
- **Top Bar:** Glass plate housing the one-line session aim input, current drift status badge, and circular user avatar framed with a 2px `#FFE3CF` border.
